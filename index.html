FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Is this a physical product?

No — the Little Bird Sitter Kit is a digital download. You’ll receive PDF files to print yourself, and nothing is shipped.

What exactly do I get?

Two PDFs: the same six pages in a US Letter version and an A4 version, so you can print whichever paper size you have.

Can I print it more than once?

Yes — print as many copies as you need for your own bird’s care. The kit is for personal use only and can’t be redistributed or resold.

Can I type into the pages?

The pages are intentionally blank so you can fill them out by hand — a paper handover that can’t run out of battery or get lost in an inbox.

Will it work for my bird?

The kit is written for companion birds. Species and routine are open fields, so it works for budgies, cockatiels, lovebirds, parrots and more.
